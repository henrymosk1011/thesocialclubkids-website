Our commitment

The Social Club works with neurodiverse kids, teens, young adults, and their families every day, and we want everyone to be able to use our website with ease. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, the standard most widely used to measure website accessibility under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

What we have done

  • Clear page structure with meaningful headings, landmarks, and a skip to main content link
  • Full keyboard access, with a visible focus indicator on every interactive element
  • Text and interface colors that meet WCAG AA contrast requirements
  • Descriptive alternative text for meaningful images
  • Contact form fields with visible labels, clear instructions, and error messages that are announced to screen readers
  • Layouts that reflow for small screens and support browser zoom and larger text settings
  • Links that open in a new tab are identified for screen reader users
  • Support for high contrast and forced colors modes

Motion and animation

Our homepage uses scroll animations. Nothing on the site flashes, and no content auto plays or loops. If you prefer less movement, turn on the reduce motion setting on your device, and the site will automatically display without scroll effects or smooth scrolling.

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings, then Accessibility, then Motion, then Reduce Motion
  • Mac: System Settings, then Accessibility, then Display, then Reduce motion
  • Windows: Settings, then Accessibility, then Visual effects, then turn off Animation effects
  • Android: Settings, then Accessibility, then Remove animations

Third party content

Some features rely on outside services, such as Calendly for scheduling and Google Maps for directions. We cannot fully control the accessibility of those services, but we are always happy to help you schedule a call or get directions by phone or email instead.

Feedback and help

We welcome your feedback. If you run into any barrier on this website, or need information in an alternative format, please let us know and we will work with you to provide what you need.
