About the club

Friendship is a skill. We make practicing it fun.

The Social Club evolved from facilitated home playdates, where a few kids come together, practice their social skills, connect through shared experiences, and cultivate meaningful relationships.

Today we run multiple age groups during the week in the after school hours, from early elementary through young adulthood, plus Social Club in School programs for grades K to 12.

Every group is small, guided, and grounded in the evidence based PEERS® curriculum, so the skills members practice here carry over to the classroom, the playground, and beyond.

The program

PEERS® based social learning groups.

PEERS® is an evidence based social skills intervention program originally developed at UCLA. In every session we tackle the real moments that shape a friendship, then practice them together in a safe, supportive group.

  • 01

    Handling rejection

    Bouncing back with confidence when things do not go as planned.

  • 02

    Conflict resolution

    Working through disagreements calmly and respectfully.

  • 03

    Staying on topic

    Following the flow of a conversation and adding to it.

  • 04

    Body language

    Reading and using non verbal communication.

  • 05

    Opening and closing

    Starting, sustaining, and ending circles of communication.

  • 06

    Sharing the conversation

    Taking turns, asking questions, and showing real interest.

  • 07

    Navigating social media

    Staying safe, kind, and connected online.

  • 08

    Self empowerment

    Building strategies to speak up and advocate for yourself.

  • 09

    Social emotional learning

    Understanding feelings and responding to others with empathy.

Meet the founder

Tamar Matossian

Tamar is a Neurodevelopmental Specialist with more than 20 years of experience working with the neurodiverse population.

She specializes in the DIR Floortime® approach and holds a Speech Language Pathology Assistant license, bringing a multidisciplinary lens to every session to support maximum development.

Tamar is the founder and director of The HummingBird Nest and The Social Club, and the author of the book What's Autism. She also leads staff trainings for schools and child facilities and provides parent coaching.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Send us a message or call (626) 765 1724.

We start with an intake session with your child to determine if they are ready for The Social Club. During the intake we also consider your child's unique personality to find the group that will be the best fit, since each group has a different dynamic.

The Social Club meets once a week for 55 minutes and is a year round program.

Parents are welcome to wait for their child in our lobby, which is where children are returned after each session.

Within 24 hours after each session, a progress note is sent to the parent's email on file through our HIPAA secured client portal.

Our clubs serve members from age 5 through 25, grouped by age and stage. We also bring Social Club in School programs to campuses for grades K to 12.

PEERS® is an evidence based social skills intervention program originally developed at UCLA. It teaches practical skills such as handling rejection, resolving conflict, staying on topic in conversation, reading body language and navigating social media.
