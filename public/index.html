About Us

The Social Club evolved from facilitated home playdates, where a few kids come together, practice their social skills, connect with one another through shared experiences, and cultivate meaningful relationships.

We currently have multiple age groups (5-18 yrs.) running during the week in the after-school hours.

The program

PEERS®-Based Social Learning Groups

PEERS® is an evidence-based social skills intervention program where we tackle topics, such as handling rejection, conflict resolution, staying on-topic during conversations, non-verbal communication, sharing the conversations, navigating social media, self-empowerment strategies, and social-emotional learning.

How it works

Getting started is simple.

  1. 01

    Book a call

    Schedule a 15 minute call to talk through your child's needs and ask anything about our groups.

  2. 02

    Intake session

    We meet your child to see if they are ready for The Social Club and get to know their unique personality.

  3. 03

    Find the right group

    Every group has a different dynamic. We place your child where they will connect and thrive.

  4. 04

    Grow every week

    Groups meet weekly for 50 minutes, year-round, with a progress note in your inbox within 48 hours.

Meet the team

A whole team in your corner.

Every club is guided by a caring team with years of hands on experience supporting neurodiverse kids and teens.

We draw on the evidence-based PEERS® curriculum and the DIR Floortime® approach, bringing a multidisciplinary lens to every session to support each member's growth.

As a part of The HummingBird Nest, we also offer parent coaching and staff trainings for schools and child facilities, so the support continues well beyond group.

  • PEERS® curriculum
  • DIR Floortime®
  • Social emotional learning
  • Friendship groups
  • Parent coaching

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Still curious? Send us a message or call (626) 765-1724.

We start with an intake session with your child to determine if they are ready for The Social Club. During the intake we will also take into consideration your child's unique personality and see which group would be the best fit, as each group has a different dynamic.

The Social Club meets once a week for 50 minutes and is a year-round program.

Parents are welcome to wait for their child in our lobby, which is where the children are returned after session.

Within 48 hours after The Social Club session, a progress note is sent to the parent's email on file with session information through our HIPAA-secured client portal.

We currently have multiple age groups (5-18 yrs.) running during the week in the after-school hours. Social Club in School is available for K-12.

PEERS® is an evidence-based social skills intervention program where we tackle topics, such as handling rejection, conflict resolution, staying on-topic during conversations, non-verbal communication, sharing the conversations, navigating social media, self-empowerment strategies, and social-emotional learning.
