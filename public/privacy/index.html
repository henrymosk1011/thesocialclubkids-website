Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how The Social Club (“The Social Club,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit thesocialclubkids.com (the “Site”) or contact us through it. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

Who we are

The Social Club is a social skills program and a part of The HummingBird Nest. We are located at 808 Foothill Blvd, Unit A, La Cañada Flintridge, CA 91011. You can reach us at contact@thesocialclubkids.com or (626) 765 1724.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you send a message through our contact form, email us, or call us, we collect the information you choose to share. This may include:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • The program you are interested in and your child's age
  • The contents of your message and any other details you decide to include

Please do not include medical, diagnostic, or other sensitive health information in the contact form or in email. If you become a client, we collect the information needed to provide services through our intake process and secure client portal instead.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, automatically processes technical information needed to deliver and protect the Site. This may include your IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, the page that referred you, the pages you request, and the date and time of your visit. We use this information to operate the Site, keep it secure, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot problems.

Information from third parties

If you book a call through Calendly, Calendly collects the information you enter in its scheduling form and shares it with us so we can hold your appointment. Calendly's handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy.

How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Respond to your questions and requests
  • Schedule calls and intake sessions, and send follow up communication you ask for
  • Understand which program may be the right fit for your child
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site
  • Detect and prevent spam, fraud, and abuse
  • Comply with our legal obligations

We do not add you to marketing email lists based on a contact form submission unless you ask us to.

Cookies and analytics

The Site does not use advertising cookies. Our security provider may set strictly necessary cookies, for example to tell the difference between people and automated bots. To protect our contact form from spam, we may use Cloudflare Turnstile, which evaluates signals from your browser to confirm that a real person is sending the form.

We may use analytics services, such as Google Analytics or Cloudflare Web Analytics, to understand how visitors use the Site, for example which pages are visited and how long visits last. These services may collect your IP address, device and browser details, and pages viewed, and Google Analytics uses cookies to do so. We review this information in aggregate to improve the Site. You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt out browser add on (opens in a new tab), and you can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services (opens in a new tab).

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how parts of the Site work.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross context behavioral advertising. We share information only in these situations:

  • Service providers that help us run the Site and respond to you, including Cloudflare (hosting, content delivery, and security), Resend (delivering contact form messages to our inbox), Calendly (scheduling), and analytics providers if enabled. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • The HummingBird Nest, our parent organization, when needed to respond to your inquiry or provide services you request.
  • Legal and safety reasons, when required by law or legal process, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of The Social Club, our clients, or others.
  • Business transfers, in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of our organization, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

Services and client records

This policy covers the Site only. If you or your child become a client, information related to services, including intake details, progress notes, and anything exchanged through our secure client portal, is handled under separate client agreements and applicable health privacy laws, including HIPAA where it applies. That information is not governed by this Website Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The Site is intended for parents, guardians, educators, and adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through the Site, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Parents and guardians may share information about their child, such as age or program interest, when contacting us. Visitors under 18 should contact us with the help of a parent or guardian.

If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information through the Site, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention

We keep contact form submissions and related correspondence only as long as needed to respond to you, to keep reasonable business records, and to meet legal obligations. After that, we delete or anonymize them. Technical logs kept by our service providers are retained according to their own schedules.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information, including encrypted HTTPS connections across the entire Site, modern browser security protections, and limited access to messages. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have provided through the Site by contacting us at contact@thesocialclubkids.com or (626) 765 1724. We will verify your request and respond within the time required by applicable law. We will never discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (together, the “CCPA”), to the extent the CCPA applies to us. These may include the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; the right to delete it; the right to correct it; the right to opt out of its sale or sharing; and the right to limit the use of sensitive personal information.

In the past 12 months, we have collected the following categories of personal information for the purposes described in this policy: identifiers (such as name, email address, phone number, and IP address), internet or network activity information (such as pages viewed), and any information you include in your message. We have disclosed these categories only to the service providers listed above. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

You may make a request yourself or through an authorized agent by contacting us using the details below. Under California Civil Code Section 1798.83, California residents may also ask whether we disclose personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes. We do not make those disclosures.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers send a Do Not Track signal. Because there is no common industry standard for responding to these signals, the Site does not currently respond to them. We do honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as a valid request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, even though we do not sell or share personal information.

Links to other sites

The Site links to other websites and services, including Calendly, Google Maps, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Amazon, and The HummingBird Nest. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please review their policies before sharing information with them. The Site is operated in the United States and is intended for visitors in the United States.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. If we make material changes, we will highlight them on the Site.
